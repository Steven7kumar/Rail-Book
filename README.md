# Rail Book
### Your Journey, Made Simple.

**Pixelcraft 2026 — UI/UX Design Challenge**  
**Selected brief:** DS-01 — Train tickets for first-time users

Rail Book is a senior-first railway booking interface concept designed primarily for passengers aged 50+ in India. It aims to make the booking journey easier to understand through clear language, large controls, predictable steps and visible support options.

## The problem

Train booking on a phone can feel overwhelming for first-time and older passengers. Dense forms, small controls, unfamiliar booking terminology and unclear waitlist or payment states can make the process difficult to follow.

The project began with a personal observation of the creator's father struggling with online train booking. This is the origin insight for the concept, not a claim of formal user research.

## The solution

Rail Book presents railway booking as a guided six-screen journey:

1. **Train Search** — enter journey details.
2. **Train Results** — compare sample train options and availability.
3. **Booking Summary** — review the selected journey and estimated fare.
4. **Passenger Details** — enter and review passenger information.
5. **Review & Payment** — check the booking and simulate a payment outcome.
6. **My Journey** — view a ticket-style journey summary and reminder options.

## UX priorities

- **Senior-first usability:** large, readable controls and straightforward labels.
- **Progressive disclosure:** focus on the current task rather than presenting every decision at once.
- **Status clarity:** availability uses text labels as well as colour.
- **Error prevention:** destination reminder, passenger-detail review and a payment-processing warning.
- **User control:** persistent Help and passenger-initiated Share actions.
- **Privacy:** sharing is user-initiated; Family Assist is optional and consent-based.

## Availability status convention

The concept uses the following status colours, always paired with text:

- Green — Available
- Light green — RAC
- Yellow — WL 1–9
- Light red — WL 10–24
- Red — WL 25+

These are illustrative status categories, not predictions or guarantees of confirmation.

## Important prototype limitations

This is a UI/UX competition prototype, not a production railway booking service.

- Train names, availability and fares are illustrative sample data.
- Payment is simulated; no real payment is processed.
- The prototype does not connect to IRCTC or live railway inventory.
- It does not issue a real ticket or PNR.
- Full multilingual translation and production voice recognition are not implemented.
